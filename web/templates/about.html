About LLM Career Matcher

Intelligent Resume-Job Matching Powered by AI

Project Overview

The LLM Career Matcher is an intelligent system that leverages advanced AI and machine learning to match job seekers with ideal career opportunities. Using Google's Gemini API and custom matching algorithms, the system analyzes resumes and jobs at multiple levels to provide highly accurate placement recommendations.

This project demonstrates the practical application of Large Language Models (LLMs) in solving real-world career placement challenges. Instead of simple keyword matching, our system understands the semantic meaning, experience requirements, and skill domains to find truly suitable matches.

How It Works

Step 1: Resume Upload

Upload your resume in PDF or text format. The system securely stores and processes your document.

Step 2: AI Analysis

Google's Gemini AI extracts and analyzes your skills, experience, and professional background.

Step 3: Matching Algorithm

Our 4-component algorithm analyzes job compatibility from multiple angles simultaneously.

Step 4: Results

Receive ranked job matches with detailed scores and component breakdowns for each opportunity.
